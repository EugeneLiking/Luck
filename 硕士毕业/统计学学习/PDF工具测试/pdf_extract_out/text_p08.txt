Stationarity
• Strict stationarity: distributions are time-invariant.
• Weak stationarity:
• E [yt] = µ
for all t
• Var (yt) = σ2 for all t
• Cov (yt, yt−j) = γj depends on j and not on t
We will mainly focus on “weak stationary time series".
